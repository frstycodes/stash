# Privacy policy

Stash doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- Stash never connects to the internet.
- It reads the folders you choose to watch (Downloads by default) on your PC, so it can show their files and thumbnails. Nothing leaves your PC.
- Your settings, such as your folder list and icon size, are saved in a file on your PC (`%APPDATA%\Stash\settings.json`). Uninstalling Stash leaves this file in place, and you can delete it.
- Stash has no analytics, crash reporting or ads.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/frstycodes/stash/issues).

Last updated: October 2026.
