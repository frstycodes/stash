# Privacy policy

Stash doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- Stash connects to the internet to download what you ask it to: the links you add, the downloads your browser hands to it, and the torrents you open. For a torrent it also talks to that torrent's trackers and to other BitTorrent users (peers), and it uploads pieces of the torrent to them while it downloads, as BitTorrent works. You can turn off seeding after a torrent finishes.
- The GitHub installer and portable versions check GitHub once a day for a newer version of Stash, and download it in the background so it's ready when you restart. The check is a plain request to GitHub's public releases page; Stash sends nothing about you or your downloads. You can turn it off in Settings, under About. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store instead.
- When the browser extension hands over a download, it includes that site's cookies and the page address, so the download works the same as it would in the browser. Stash sends them only to that site, and only for that download.
- Stash reads the folders you choose to watch (Downloads by default) on your PC, so it can show their files and thumbnails. None of that leaves your PC.
- Your settings and your list of downloads are saved in `%APPDATA%\Stash` on your PC. Uninstalling Stash leaves this folder in place, and you can delete it.
- Stash has no analytics, crash reporting or ads, and no server of its own.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/frstycodes/stash/issues).
